Our kitchen

A cuisine that crosses the Atlantic,
without losing its accent.

Peruvian cuisine is mixed-heritage by nature: Andean technique, Pacific produce, and the legacy of those who arrived later — Italian, Chinese, Japanese. At TUMI we cook from that blend, with the respect every recipe deserves and the discipline of working only with fresh market produce.

From ceviche cut to order to meat that spends hours over a slow flame, every dish leaves the kitchen when it's ready — not before. That's how we see it at TUMI, and that's how we serve it, dish by dish, in the heart of Mallorca.

"We don't translate Peruvian cuisine. We serve it exactly as it is."
